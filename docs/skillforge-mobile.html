Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

SkillTracker ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

Account data: When you register, we collect your name and email address. Passwords are stored as one-way bcrypt hashes — we can never read your password.

Usage data: Skill logs (skill name, hours, notes, date), goals, and streak data that you create inside the app.

Device data: We may collect anonymous device type and OS version for crash reporting. We do not collect location, contacts, camera, or microphone data.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve SkillTracker features — syncing your logs across devices, calculating streaks, and generating progress reports.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

3. Offline-First & Data Storage

SkillTracker stores all your data locally on your device first. When you are online, your data is synced to our secure servers hosted on Railway (PostgreSQL). Local data is never deleted after sync.

All data transmitted between the app and server is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS 1.2+.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen. On deletion, all your personal data (logs, goals, skills, streak history) is permanently erased from our servers within 30 days.

5. Third-Party Services

SkillTracker currently uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Future integrations (e.g. Google OAuth) will be disclosed and require your explicit consent.

6. Children's Privacy

SkillTracker is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data at any time. Use the Export Data option in Profile settings to download your data. To request deletion, use the Sign Out → Delete Account option.

8. Contact

For any privacy concerns, contact us at: privacy@skilltracker.app
